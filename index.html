About Rookery Chess, a free chess game

Rookery Chess is a free chess game that runs in your browser. Play chess vs computer at five difficulty levels, or play a friend on the same device. It was built by Hari Deevagan in Chennai, India.

Chess game features

  • Play chess vs computer: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert levels, as White, Black or a random side.
  • Two-player chess: multiplayer on one device, taking turns on the same board.
  • Computer vs computer: watch two AI players face each other.
  • Chess clocks: bullet, blitz and rapid time controls, or a custom time and increment.
  • Puzzles: mate in 1, 2 and 3, best-move and tactical puzzles.
  • Analysis: evaluation bar and engine lines for any game or position.
  • FEN and PGN: import, copy and download positions and games.
  • Free and private: no account needed, and it works on phones and desktops.

Chess game FAQ

Is Rookery Chess free?

Yes. It is completely free to play, with no account, no sign-up and no ads.

Can I play chess vs computer?

Yes. Choose Player vs AI and pick a difficulty: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. You can play White, Black or a random side.

Is there multiplayer chess?

There is a two-player mode for two people on the same device, taking turns on one board. Online matchmaking over the internet is not available.

Can I set a chess clock?

Yes. Pick a bullet, blitz or rapid time control, or enter your own minutes and increment, or play with no clock.

Are there chess puzzles and analysis?

Yes. Solve mate-in-1, mate-in-2, mate-in-3 and best-move puzzles, and analyse any game or position with an evaluation bar.

Can I import and export games?

Yes. You can load and copy positions as FEN and import, copy or download games as PGN.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is mobile-first and works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and desktops.

Who made this chess game?
